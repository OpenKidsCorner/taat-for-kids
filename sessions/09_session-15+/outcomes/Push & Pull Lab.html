Force words

Push

A force that moves something away from you.

Pull

A force that moves something toward you.

Friction

A force that slows things down when two surfaces rub. Rough surfaces like gravel and sand have lots of friction. Smooth ice has very little.

Gravity

A pull toward the Earth. It makes things fall, and pulls them back down a hill.

Magnetic force

A magnet pulls iron and steel things, even without touching them.

Scientists measure force in newtons (N). Braking a car takes thousands of newtons. Pushing a door open takes about 20 N.
